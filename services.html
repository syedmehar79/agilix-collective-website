PROCESS

how we build

Five stages, one loop. We don’t freeze the spec — we keep the product in motion.

  1. 01

    discover

    Name the outcome, the users, and the constraints before a line of code is sized.

  2. 02

    plan

    Architecture, scope, and a delivery rhythm you can track week by week.

  3. 03

    build

    Agile sprints with demos, reviews, and course-correction in the open.

  4. 04

    launch

    Deploy, document, and hand over so the product is usable on day one.

  5. 05

    improve

    Monitor, support, and iterate against real usage — not a frozen spec.
